CJCHT Forests Policy Announcement

Date: March 15, 2024 (Aligned with main policy)

Title: CJCHT's Commitment to a Deforestation-Free Supply Chain Introduction: CJCHT is committed to ensuring that our value chain is free from deforestation and the conversion of natural ecosystems. Recognizing that key commodities in the footwear and packaging industries—specifically timber products (paper/cartons), leather, and natural rubber—can be drivers of forest loss, this policy outlines our strategic approach to responsible sourcing and supply chain management. This commitment is a cornerstone of our broader environmental stewardship and our promise to protect vital forest ecosystems.

Key Commitments:

  1. Commitment to No-Deforestation and No-Conversion: We are committed to a deforestation- and conversion-free supply chain for all the commodities we source. We work with our suppliers to ensure that the raw materials in our products do not originate from land deforested or converted after December 31, 2020.
  2. Traceability and Transparency: We are dedicated to achieving full traceability for our high-risk commodities. Our core strategy involves implementing robust traceability systems to map our supply chain, from the finished product back to the material's origin, to verify its deforestation-free status.
  3. Prioritizing Certified Sourcing: We mandate a preference for materials from certified sources that provide assurance of responsible forest management. This includes:
    • Timber Products: Prioritizing the use of Forest Stewardship Council (FSC) certified paper and board for our packaging.
    • Leather: Prioritizing sourcing from tanneries certified by the Leather Working Group (LWG), which promotes environmental best practices and traceability.
  4. Supplier Engagement and Requirements: Compliance with this policy is a mandatory requirement for our strategic suppliers. Through our supplier engagement programs, we provide training and support to help our partners build the capacity needed to meet our traceability and certification standards.
  5. Respect for Human Rights: Our commitment to protecting forests is intrinsically linked to respecting the rights of Indigenous Peoples and local communities. We require our suppliers to operate in a manner that respects land rights and promotes positive social outcomes.

Conclusion: CJCHT's Forests Policy is integral to our identity as a sustainability leader. We are dedicated to working collaboratively with our customers, suppliers, and partners to create a value chain that protects and values the world's forests for future generations.
