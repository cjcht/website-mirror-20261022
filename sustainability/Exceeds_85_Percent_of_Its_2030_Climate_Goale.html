CJCHT Exceeds 85% of its 2030 Climate Goal, Demonstrating Significant Progress on Science-Based Targets

Company achieves a 37.4% reduction in emissions from its 2020 baseline, driven by renewable energy adoption and value chain innovation.

Taipei ‒ September 16, 2025 ‒ CJCHT Groups, a leading sustainability solutions provider in the global footwear supply chain, today announced significant progress towards its validated Science-Based Targets (SBTi). For the 2024 reporting period, the company achieved an absolute reduction of around 37.4% in its Scope 1 and 2 greenhouse gas (GHG) emissions from its 2020 baseline year. This accomplishment means CJCHT has already achieved 89% of its ambitious 2030 target of a 42% reduction, underscoring its commitment to a 1.5°C future and its 2050 net-zero ambition.

"Our progress is a testament to our strategy of embedding sustainability deep within the value chain," said Raymond Wang, Senior Sustainability Director of CJCHT Groups. "We are not just managing our own footprint; we are building a collaborative ecosystem with our partners to drive systemic change. By leveraging our proprietary LCA data platforms and co-innovating on low-carbon products, we are demonstrating that a resilient, decarbonized supply chain is not only possible but is also a powerful driver of business growth and value creation for our customers."

CJCHT's climate transition plan, which received SBTi validation in 2021, is built on a foundation of tangible action and data-driven strategy. Key achievements in the 2024 reporting year that contributed to this progress include:

• Accelerated Renewable Energy Adoption: A core component of the company's decarbonization strategy, on-site solar PV installations at key supplier facilities resulted in an estimated avoidance of 832 metric tons of CO2e in Scope 2 emissions. This initiative is a key driver towards the company's target, set in 2022, of achieving 30% renewable energy consumption.

• Deep Value Chain Energy Efficiency: The company's Equipment Modernization program, which targets high-consumption machinery like air compressors and injection molding machines, achieved an additional 609 metric tons of CO2e in annual emissions savings.

• Market Leadership in Low-Carbon Products: The company saw explosive growth in its innovative sustainable product lines. In packaging, 10.71% of total materials by weight now come from circular sources, including 100% PCR plastic hangers and polybags containing 30% food-waste-derived shell powder.

• Industry Recognition: The success of this integrated strategy was validated by key partners. In June 2025, CJCHT was awarded the 2024 Sustainability Recognition by Walmart Canada, acknowledging the company's leadership and tangible impact in helping its customers achieve their own ambitious climate goals.

CJCHT remains focused on its long-term vision, continuing to invest in R&D for next-generation bio-materials and expanding its digital "ERP+ESG" transformation initiative with partners like the A1 Smart Factory Alliance and SSBTi.org to build a truly transparent and accountable net-zero value chain.

About CJCHT Groups:

CJCHT Groups is a leading sustainability solutions provider within the global footwear supply chain, specializing in design, development, and strategic supply chain management. Guided by its "ClearCarbon" philosophy and leveraging proprietary data platforms, CJCHT collaborates with the world's leading retailers and brands to create innovative, low-carbon products and build resilient, transparent, and sustainable value chains.
