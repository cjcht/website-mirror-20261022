CJCHT Water Stewardship Policy Announcement

Date: March 15, 2024 (Aligned with main policy)

Title: CJCHT's Commitment to Water Stewardship and Supply Chain Resilience Introduction: CJCHT recognizes that water is a critical shared resource and that responsible water management is essential for the health of ecosystems, communities, and our business. This policy outlines our strategic approach to water stewardship, focusing on minimizing our water footprint, eliminating pollution, and building resilience in our value chain, particularly within the water-stressed river basins where we operate.

Key Commitments:

  1. Context-Based Water Management: We acknowledge that water is a local issue. Our strategy is therefore context-based, prioritizing action in the river basins where our operations have the most significant dependencies and impacts. Based on our risk assessments, we focus our primary efforts on our key production hubs in the Min River and Yangtze River basins.
  2. Water Efficiency and Circularity: We are committed to reducing water consumption throughout our manufacturing processes. We work with our suppliers to invest in water- saving technologies and promote the adoption of water reuse and recycling systems, moving towards circular water management to minimize dependency on local freshwater sources.
  3. Water Quality and Chemical Management: Protecting water quality is paramount. Our policy mandates the elimination of hazardous chemicals from our supply chain. We have formally adopted the ZDHC (Zero Discharge of Hazardous Chemicals) Programme, including its Manufacturing Restricted Substances List (MRSL) and Wastewater Guidelines, as our core standard for ensuring the quality of wastewater discharged from our partner facilities.
  4. Supplier Engagement and Resilience: We require our strategic suppliers located in high-risk water basins to actively manage their water use. This includes conducting regular water audits, setting context-appropriate water targets, and developing robust response plans for water-related physical risks like scarcity and flooding.

Conclusion: CJCHT's Water Stewardship Policy reflects our commitment to being a responsible water user and a resilient business partner. By embedding these principles into our supply chain management, we aim to protect shared water resources and ensure the long-term sustainability of our operations and the communities we rely on.
