CJCHT Groups Limited Recognized as Four-Time CDP Supplier Engagement Leader

CJCHT Groups Limited is pleased to announce that it has, for the fourth time, been recognized as a Supplier Engagement Leader — the highest designation in the CDP Supplier Engagement Assessment (SEA), an annual evaluation by CDP, the international non-governmental organization that operates the world's leading environmental disclosure system.

CJCHT achieved SEA Leader status in the 2021, 2022, 2024, and 2025 assessment cycles, and has maintained consistent A or A− scoring across every cycle since 2021. (In 2021 and 2022, the award was issued under CDP's predecessor designation "Supplier Engagement Rating, SER.")

The CDP Supplier Engagement Assessment evaluates a company's supply chain engagement on climate change. Companies are scored on responses across four areas of the CDP Climate Change Questionnaire — Governance, Targets, Value Chain (Scope 3), and Value Chain Engagement — together with their overall Climate Change Questionnaire assessment.

For over a decade, CJCHT has invested in a supplier data infrastructure designed specifically for supply-chain decarbonization: a proprietary WeChat-based reporting application that collects standardized environmental data from our seven core supplier factories and broader tier-1 supply network, combined with sustainability terms embedded in our purchasing covenants. This infrastructure is the operational basis for our Scope 3 inventory, our customer-allocated emissions disclosures to Walmart and Target under the Project Gigaton program, and our third-party verified GHG accounting under ISO 14064-1 and ISO 14064-3.

"Four SEA Leader designations across five cycles is not a coincidence — it is the receipt of work that began long before any of these scores were issued," commented Raymond Wang, Chief Sustainability Officer. "Over a decade ago we made a decision to use our purchasing relationships not only to source quality footwear, but to build a shared data backbone for climate disclosure across our supply network. CDP's standards become more demanding every year, and our supply partners continue to meet them. This recognition belongs to our suppliers as much as to CJCHT."

CJCHT remains committed to working with its supply network to reduce environmental impact across the full footwear value chain.

About CJCHT Groups Limited

CJCHT Groups Limited is a sustainability solutions provider within the global footwear supply chain, headquartered in Shanghai with operations across East China, South China, and Putian. CJCHT delivers design, development, and strategic supply chain management for major international footwear retailers — including Walmart and Target — and is a recognized leader in low-carbon footwear innovation through its ZEO product family.

CDP is a not-for-profit organization that runs the world's leading environmental disclosure system. Learn more at www.cdp.net.
